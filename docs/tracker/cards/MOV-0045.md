## Why this exists

A household collaborator viewing a safe image, an AI reading private evidence,
and an AI permanently adding bytes are three different promises. Sharing the
move must not silently share every attachment or original.

## Current truth

Moving enforces move grants and roles and has a compatibility policy switch for
photo writes. It does not yet have the final canonical media-write scope or a
documented mapping from Moving roles to the family Private/Trusted/Unlisted/
Public media semantics.

## Next safe action

Implement ordinary `moving.media.read` and, subject to Scott's Ready ruling,
the recommended raised `moving.media.write`. Map existing roles to explicit
view-only Trusted access, keep original download separate, make link inclusion
per attachment, and require human rights/publish action for Public.

## Definition of done

- [ ] Read never implies write, original download, publish, or another move.
- [ ] Product scopes stay out of OAuth `scopes_supported` identity scopes.
- [ ] Revocation/expiry affects the next discovery, call, upload finish, and
      attachment; selected-move grants are checked on every target.
- [ ] Derivatives are trusted-server generated and stripped of EXIF/GPS.
- [ ] Both MCP dialects preserve pre-grant capability/discovery behavior and
      distinguish token 401 from authorization-service 503.
- [ ] Grant-lifecycle regression tests cover resurrection, union, sentinel
      identity, expired starvation, and shared issuer normalization.

## Constraints

Keep sign-in-as-approval and existing move roles unless Scott changes them.
Never let that ruling imply raised media write, original download, participant
change, permanent delete, public publishing, or access to another move.

## Completion evidence

Not started. Raised media write is a recommendation awaiting the Work Order's
Ready ruling, not a Current capability or approved security decision.
